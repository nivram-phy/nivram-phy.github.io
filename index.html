Research overview

My research seeks to understand how the strong nuclear force shapes the properties of matter in our universe. I am particularly interested in the emergence of collective phenomena when gluons, the particles that mediate this force, reach extraordinarily high densities inside atomic nuclei, and how their interactions give rise to a new regime of matter known as gluon saturation.

To investigate these fundamental questions, I work with the ALICE experiment at CERN, developing experimental techniques, particle detectors and computational methods to probe the internal structure of nuclear matter.

For more details on my ongoing research projects, see here.

I am also a Professor of Practice at SASTRA University, where I teach courses in nuclear technologies.
