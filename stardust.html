The Stardust Collective

The Stardust Collective is a science-outreach project I created for children and families. It is built around physical postcards: one idea in physics, a letter from a working scientist, and an invitation for children to write back with their own questions.

The project grew out of my interest in creating slower, tactile ways for children to encounter science outside a classroom.
